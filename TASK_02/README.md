# Task 2 – Phishing Awareness Program

A self-contained, web-based training module (`index.html`, no dependencies, works offline – just open it in a browser).

## Contents
1. What phishing is and why it works
2. Types: bulk, spear, whaling/BEC, smishing, vishing, quishing
3. How to identify suspicious emails, fake login pages and fraudulent websites (comparison table)
4. **Interactive practice** – click the 6 red flags in a sample (fictional) phishing email
5. Social engineering techniques (urgency, authority, fear, greed, pretexting, baiting, tailgating)
6. **Four case studies** – fake supplier invoices (Google/Facebook), fake security alert (2016 US election hack), 2020 Twitter phone-phishing breach, the everyday KYC SMS scam
7. Practical security tips and what to do if you clicked
8. **10-question quiz** with explanations and a score

All brands in the practice email are fictional.

## Hosting
Open `index.html` locally, or publish the folder on GitHub Pages (Settings → Pages) to share a live link on LinkedIn.
